# Data Retention and Deletion

## Defaults
Prompt logs are retained 30 days, metrics 13 months, and billing records 7 years. Customers on Enterprise can configure shorter prompt retention. Deleted workspaces enter a 14-day soft-delete period.

## Customer content
Uploaded corpora remain until the customer deletes them or the workspace is purged. Derived embeddings are deleted with the source document. Cached KV artifacts, when enabled, follow the same invalidation rules as source hashes.

## Legal holds
Legal holds suspend deletion for specified objects. Holds are visible to workspace admins. Export tools remain available during a hold. Removing a hold resumes the ordinary retention clock.

## Deletion requests
Privacy deletion requests are tracked as tickets with a 30-day completion target. Completion evidence includes object counts removed and verification queries. Backups age out on their own schedule and are not selectively edited.
