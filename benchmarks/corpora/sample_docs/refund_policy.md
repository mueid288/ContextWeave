# Refund and Returns Policy

## Overview
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase for software subscriptions and within 14 days for professional services, subject to the conditions below. This policy applies to direct purchases from Acme Cloud Console and does not override mandatory consumer protections in the customer's jurisdiction.

## Eligibility
Refunds are available when the service experienced material downtime exceeding the SLA credit threshold, when the purchased SKU was provisioned incorrectly by Acme, or when the customer cancels within the cooling-off window before any production workload is created. Chargebacks initiated without contacting support first may delay remediation.

## Process
Open a ticket from the billing portal, include the invoice id, workspace id, and a short description of the issue. Finance acknowledges within one business day. Approved refunds settle to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Store credit is offered when the original method cannot be refunded.

## Non-refundable items
Marketplace third-party listings, consumed prepaid inference credits, and custom implementation retainers after kickoff are non-refundable except where required by law. Promotional credits expire and are never paid out as cash.

## Disputes
If a refund is denied, the customer may escalate to a billing review within 14 days. Escalations require written rationale and any relevant logs. Final decisions are communicated by email.
