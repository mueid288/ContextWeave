# Service Level Agreement — Uptime

## Commitment
Acme targets 99.9% monthly uptime for the Inference API in regions marked Generally Available. Scheduled maintenance windows announced 72 hours in advance do not count against uptime if they remain inside the published window.

## Credits
If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, customers receive service credits: 10% of monthly fees below 99.9%, 25% below 99.0%, and 50% below 95.0%. Credits apply to future invoices only and require a request within 30 days after the month closes.

## Exclusions
Downtime caused by customer misconfiguration, upstream model provider outages outside Acme control, force majeure, or beta features is excluded. Partial degradation that still serves successful requests above the error threshold may not qualify.

## Measurement
Uptime is measured from Acme's multi-region synthetic probes every 60 seconds. Customer-side VPN or DNS failures are out of scope. Incident timelines are published in the status history.
