# Incident Response Runbook

## Severities
SEV-1: customer-wide outage or data exposure. SEV-2: major feature down for many tenants. SEV-3: degraded performance. SEV-4: minor bug.

## Roles
Incident commander, communications lead, and operations lead are assigned in the first 10 minutes. Engineers join the bridge and capture timelines in the incident doc.

## Mitigation
Prefer disable switches and traffic drains over risky hotfixes during SEV-1. For cache poisoning suspicions, disable cross-request KV reuse and force full prefill until audit completes.

## Postmortems
Blameless postmortems are due within 5 business days for SEV-1/2. Action items need owners and dates. Customer-facing summaries omit internal hostnames and credentials.
